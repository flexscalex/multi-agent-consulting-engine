# Engagement Brief

This is the single source of truth for the engagement. The example below is a generic
market-entry question. Replace everything under "The Brief" with your own situation.

## The Brief

**Client:** A mid-sized North American specialty coffee roaster (roughly $40M annual
revenue, wholesale and a small DTC channel) that currently sells whole bean and ground
coffee to grocery and cafe accounts.

**The decision:** The client is considering entering the ready-to-drink (RTD) cold brew
category in cans, sold through grocery and convenience retail. They want an outside view
on whether to do it, and if so, how.

**What we need answered:**
- Is the RTD cold brew category still growing, or is it saturating? What does the current
  data say about size, growth rate, and competitive concentration?
- What is the right entry strategy: build in-house production, co-pack with a partner, or
  acquire a small existing RTD brand? What are the trade-offs?
- What does a credible first pilot look like, with realistic unit economics, a target
  retail footprint, funding needs, and a 12 to 24 month milestone plan?
- What are the biggest risks (margin pressure, retail slotting fees, shelf-life and cold
  chain, incumbent response) and how have comparable brands handled them?

**Constraints:**
- The client wants to protect its premium brand and wholesale relationships.
- Capital is limited; a heavy upfront capex build is a hard sell without a clear payback.
- The recommendation must be executable by a lean team within 18 months.

**What a great answer looks like:** A clear recommended direction backed by current,
citable market data, an honest read on what is provable versus uncertain, a concrete
fundable pilot, and a frank account of how this could fail.
